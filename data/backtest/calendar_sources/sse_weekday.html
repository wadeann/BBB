      Trading Schedule

        SSE Trading Calendar

        2025

        The SSE is open for trading Monday through Friday, with the exception of public holidays and other closing days declared by the Exchange in advance. The SSE market will be closed on the days listed below for 2025.


        New Year's Day January 1, 2025 (Wednesday)
        Chinese New Year January 28 (Tuesday) - February 4 (Tuesday), plus January 26 (Sunday) and February 8 (Saturday)
        Qingming Festival
        (also known as Tomb-Sweeping Day)        		 April 4 (Friday) - April 6 (Sunday)
        Chinese Labor Day May 1 (Thursday) - May 5 (Monday), plus April 27 (Sunday)
        Dragon Boat Festival May 31 (Saturday) - June 2 (Monday)
        China's National Day and Mid-Autumn Festival October 1 (Wednesday) - October 8 (Wednesday), plus September 28 (Sunday) and October 11 (Saturday)

        2024

        The SSE is open for trading Monday through Friday, with the exception of public holidays and other closing days declared by the Exchange in advance. The SSE market will be closed on the days listed below for 2024.


        New Year's Day December 30, 2023 (Saturdy) - January 1, 2024 (Monday)
        Chinese New Year February 9 (Friday) - February 17 (Saturdy), plus February 4 (Sunday) and February 18 (Sunday)
        Qingming Festival
        (also known as Tomb-Sweeping Day)        		 April 4 (Thursday) - April 6 (Saturdy), plus April 7 (Sunday)
        Chinese Labor Day May 1 (Wednesday) - May 5 (Sunday), plus April 28 (Sunday) and May 11 (Saturday)
        Dragon Boat Festival June 10 (Monday)
        Mid-Autumn Festival September 15 (Sunday) - September 17 (Tuesday), plus September 14 (Saturday)
        China National Day October 1 (Tuesday) - October 7(Monday), plus September 29 (Sunday) and October 12 (Saturday)
